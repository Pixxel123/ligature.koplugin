Contractions (in words.buckets.tsv and words.popular.tsv)

The word list had no words with an apostrophe. tools/add_contractions.py
added 68 English contractions ("don't", "I'm", "they're", ...) at the
frequencies of wordfreq 3.1.1 (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), whose
word frequency data is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The list and its
frequencies are in tools/contractions_en.tsv.

Word-pair table (words.pairs.tsv, words.pairs.idx)

Counted from the English sentences of Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org),
export of 2026-09-25, by tools/build_word_pairs.py. Tatoeba's sentences
are the work of its contributors and are licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence (CC BY 2.0 FR,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). The table holds counts
derived from them, not the sentences themselves.

Capitals and added words (words.buckets.tsv, words.popular.tsv)

Counted from the eng_news_2024_1M, eng-com_web-public_2018_1M, eng_wikipedia_2016_1M corpora of the Leipzig Corpora
Collection (https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de), licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), by
tools/build_dictionary.py: its words, their frequencies and
spellings, not its sentences.

Words that neither Wiktionary (https://www.wiktionary.org, via
https://kaikki.org) nor the corpora above know were left out;
no Wiktionary text is included.
