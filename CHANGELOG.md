# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release as Ligature, which supersedes Tapless 0.4.0.

- far more swipes recognised: long words by the shape of the whole swipe,
  swipes that start on the next key, cut corners and double letters
- learns the words you use, word pairs for each language and where your
  thumb lands
- word completions while tapping
- one-handed keyboard that moves, resizes and remembers its place
- languages picked on first run: English comes with the plugin, the other
  10 are downloaded
- thin grey swipe trail that also shows in night mode
- hold a suggestion to block it; offensive words ranked down
- contractions typed with their apostrophes
- backspace after a swipe deletes the whole word; slide left to delete more
- a second finger no longer cuts a swipe short

## 0.4.0

- personalized dictionaries
- loops for repeated letters
- geometric re-ranking
- correct capitalization
- minor fixes

## 0.3.0

- support for additional languages (Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Danish, Czech, Turkish)
- dictionary manager
- improved performance
