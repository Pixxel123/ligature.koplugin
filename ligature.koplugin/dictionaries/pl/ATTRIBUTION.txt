Ligature dictionary data
========================

This package contains the indexed dictionary shipped with
Tapless (https://github.com/azac/tapless.koplugin). The source project and data
licensing information are documented in the included files.

Capitals and added words (words.buckets.tsv, words.popular.tsv)

Counted from the pol_news_2024_1M, pol_wikipedia_2021_1M corpora of the Leipzig Corpora
Collection (https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de), licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), by
tools/build_dictionary.py: its words, their frequencies and
spellings, not its sentences.

Words that neither Wiktionary (https://www.wiktionary.org, via
https://kaikki.org) nor the corpora above know were left out;
no Wiktionary text is included.
